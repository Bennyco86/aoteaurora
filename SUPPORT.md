# AOTEAURORA support

AOTEAURORA is a commercial New Zealand-focused southern-lights app priced at USD 8.99. It is developed, published and operated by **AOTEAURORA LIMITED**. Its Google Play listing is in progress, and an Apple App Store release is planned.

## Get help

- [Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/Bennyco86/aoteaurora/issues/new) for a reproducible bug or feature idea.
- Email `aoteauroranz@gmail.com` for a private support question.
- Use the [visual support guide](support.html) for common troubleshooting steps.

Include what you expected, what happened, the approximate New Zealand date and time, your device/browser or build, and a screenshot with personal information removed.

Do not post precise home coordinates, passwords, private preview links or personal information in a public issue.

## Important limitations

- A conditions rating is not a guaranteed aurora sighting or a calculated probability.
- Third-party scientific and weather feeds can be delayed, revised or unavailable.
- Notifications are planned but disabled in the current release candidate.
- This repository contains no direct installer or app download. Official store links will be published on the website when live.

See the [privacy notice](PRIVACY.md) for information about optional location access and local storage.
